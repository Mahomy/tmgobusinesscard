01

Turn Your Vehicle Into an Income-Generating Asset

Make your vehicle work for you.

Do you own a vehicle? Are you employed full-time, running a business or simply looking for an additional income opportunity?

TM Go SA is inviting vehicle owners to join its innovative e-hailing platform. The initiative aims to create opportunities for people to earn additional income from their vehicles while helping address transportation needs in South Africa.

Our vision: Turn privately owned vehicles into income-generating assets while creating employment opportunities for fellow South Africans.
02

Reaching Rural Communities

Connecting people to essential transport services.

TM Go SA is committed to extending transport services to rural and underserved communities where reliable transportation can make a meaningful difference.

We appeal especially to owners of suitable vehicles to become part of this initiative.

Vehicles We Welcome

  • Land Rovers
  • Toyota Hilux vehicles
  • Jeeps
  • Ford vans
  • Other suitable vehicles

Communities We Aim to Serve

  • Senior citizens
  • Pensioners
  • Rural communities
  • People in underserved areas
  • Residents requiring access to essential services

Access to Essential Services

Help communities access reliable transport to:

  • Clinics and hospitals.
  • Healthcare centres.
  • Other essential community services.
Make a difference: Your vehicle can generate income while helping someone access essential services and improving mobility in rural areas.
